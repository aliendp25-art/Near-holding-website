ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Understanding NEAR Holding and the NEAR Blockchain

NEAR Holding is a digital asset holding platform built around the NEAR blockchain ecosystem. It provides an information and access point for users who want to explore structured digital asset holding options through a Telegram Mini App.

The NEAR blockchain is designed to support decentralized applications and digital asset activity. NEAR is the network's native token. Before interacting with any digital asset platform, users should understand the network, wallet requirements, transaction fees and the risks associated with cryptocurrency.

Explore NEAR Holding Package Options

The NEAR Holding Mini App presents package amounts of 10, 50, 100, 150, 200 and 300 NEAR. These amounts represent the available package structure displayed on this website. Users should open the Mini App to review the current availability, eligibility requirements and complete terms before submitting a request.

The stated holding period is 30 days. Users should carefully review the applicable conditions, including the start of the holding period, request review procedures, maturity conditions and any withdrawal restrictions. The information shown inside the Mini App should be checked before taking action.

Using a Compatible NEAR Wallet

A compatible NEAR wallet allows users to interact with applications on the NEAR network. Wallet owners are responsible for protecting their recovery phrases and private keys. Never share these credentials with another person or enter them into an untrusted website. Always verify the destination and network before approving a blockchain transaction.

Access NEAR Holding Through Telegram

The NEAR Holding Telegram Mini App provides access to the platform interface. Users can explore the available package information, review instructions and follow the applicable request process. The official Telegram channel shares platform announcements and educational updates.
